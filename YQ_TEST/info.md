# YouthQuake admin registration system.

## General info and context
Youthquake is a national methodist camp occuring once every 4 year and held over 5 days. inviting participants from all over malaysia, with around 200-300 participants. The admin registration team needed a way to process and to manage participants during registration and overall day to day info. 

## Proposal
Create a youthquake admin registration system where user can quickly process incoming participants and built to streamline the process flow.

## Usual process flow:
 1. Registered Participants list and info are stored in excel sheets.
 2. on the day itself the excel sheet is printed out on large A2 paper.
 3. Participants line up to register to enter the camp on the day itself.
 3. Registration staff will check the names on the paper with a long ruler and identify the participants info such as "shirt size" , "names" etc. 
 4. once found the team will request the participants to sign their names and hand in their phones (phone is not allowed for the duration of the camp and has to be safely kept by organizer). 
 5. the phone information will be written down on the sheet to keep track of it. 
 6. shirts and camp info pack will be given to the participants.
 7. Registration complete


## System feature. 
1. Will Provide an interface to search registered participants and provide all neccesary info of the participants.
    - Names
    - Age
    - Church origin
    - Gender
    - Shirt size
    - signed up workshops
    - Devotion Group.
    - Team building groups.
    - Remarks (food allergies etc or special requirements)
    - emergency contact

2. Will handle and track participants registration and help ease the flow of the registration.
3. Provide useful dashboard etc. 
4. Able to export as reports.
