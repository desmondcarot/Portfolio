Who We Are

About MYF

The Methodist Youth Fellowship (MYF) is the youth arm of Emmanuel Methodist Church — a space for young people to come together every Sunday after service. Whether you're exploring faith, building friendships, or looking for a place to belong, MYF is that place.

Our Vision

"EMCMYF aligns with the Great Commission by becoming mature Christian youths for God's Kingdom and People."

🌱

Grow

Deepening faith and spiritual maturity through discipleship and the Word.

🤝

Connect

Building genuine community across schools, backgrounds, and life stages.

🌍

Serve

Moving beyond PJ to reach and impact others for God's Kingdom.

First Time Here?

You'll fit right in.

Every person in MYF was once new. Here's everything you need — which is honestly not much.

Will I know anyone?

Don't worry — we all started that way.

Most of us walked in not knowing a single person. MYF is a welcoming bunch — someone will find you before you even start looking.

What should I bring?

Just yourself.

No Bible, no notebook, no prep needed. Come as you are, with an open heart — that's genuinely all you need.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Nope. Just show up.

No homework, no prior knowledge required. Just join in and enjoy the experience. We figure the rest out together.

Is it only for church members?

Everyone is welcome here.

Whether you're a regular churchgoer, curious about faith, or just tagged along with a friend — MYF is open to all. Come as you are.
